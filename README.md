	**FEATURES**

1\. Nav bar: It Shows the Restaurants logo and five hyperlinks that scroll down to various sections of the landing page when clicked and on the right of the hyperlink is another hyperlink that looks like a dark mode button when clicked on it changes the page’s background color and the color of some of the text, the button then disappears and is replaced with another button (hyperlink) that looks like a button for light mode which when clicked on reverts the page back to its former background color and text color. It basically reverts the page back to how it originally was and lastly the Plan a party button (hyperlink) takes the webpage visitor to the contact us section so they can contact us to plan a party.

2\. Hero Section: A simple hero section which shows the restaurant's slogan and talks a little about the restaurant while advertising a picture of a drink that is served at the restaurant.

3\. Our Services Section: Another Section which shows a 2 by 3 grid showing the restaurant's services and talking about it and when you hover over the service , box-shadow disappears and the background color and the text color transition and switch.

4.How we work Section: Nothing much here, some basic text with an orange background and a black \<hr\> with 3 columns explaining how they can reach out to use the restaurant's services and pick the services they want.

5.Recent Work Section: It shows some basic text that shows our recent works or our latest creations. It then advertises some of the services talked about before in the services section through the use of images.

6.About Us Section: Just give the visitor some basic info about the restaurant. It ends with a small banner

7.Contact Us Section: Here there is some basic text on the left that gives the customer a reason on why they would want to contact us while providing information on where they can find us and a number they can call us on to discuss any service , it also has a form on its right where they customer could fill in their info and reach out to us about a specific service they are interested in with a send enquiry button that lowkey doesn’t do anything it just changes opacity when hovered on and acts as if it was clicked when its clicked on (box-shadow).  
8\. Footer: I don’t consider it a section but it shows some copyright info about the restaurant’s website and it has some hyperlink on the far right that work just like the ones in the nav bar at the top of the page.
